## Adding your own

Add the file under `models/` or `splats/`, add an entry to [`catalog.json`](catalog.json) (the
key is the name you say to Muse; lowercase letters, digits, `-`, `_`), and list its license here.
Only add assets you hold the rights to redistribute.
